About PERSONA

PERSONA is a reader-first storytelling space. Stories can be personal, fictional, mysterious, funny, unfinished, or completely unexpected. The idea is simple: people have stories, and stories deserve people who will listen.

This version is fully interactive in your browser. Stories open in their own reading view, ratings and comments work locally, and the writing form can create a new story on the page.
